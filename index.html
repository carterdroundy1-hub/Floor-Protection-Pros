Our Protection System

A layered approach built for active custom-home jobsites.

01

Walk the Project

We identify high-traffic areas, finished surfaces, stairs, landings, and vulnerable transitions.

02

Install the Right Materials

We use Floorotex, cardboard, duct tape where appropriate, and no-residue edge tape based on the surface and situation.

03

Keep Crews Moving

Protection is installed so work can continue while finished floors stay covered and presentable.

04

Maintain the Standard

Optional service visits keep protection repaired, clean, and secure throughout the build.
